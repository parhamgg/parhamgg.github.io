About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at McGill University, working at the intersection of computational biology, artificial intelligence, and citizen science. I am completing my doctorate under the supervision of Jérôme Waldispühl.

My research leverages large-scale citizen science to drive AI-powered advances in computational biology, with a current focus on microbial sequence alignment, phylogenetics, and gut biomarker discovery.

My work is built around the citizen science project Borderlands Science, which is a puzzle game embedded within Borderlands 3. It has collected over 130 million puzzle solutions from 4 million players and was published in Nature Biotechnology in 2024 — demonstrating that large-scale human contributions can meaningfully advance biological research.

I previously earned my BSc in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.
