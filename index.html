About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at McGill University, working at the intersection of bioinformatics, artificial intelligence, and citizen science, supervised by Jérôme Waldispühl and co-supervised by Mathieu Blanchette.

My research integrates algorithmic and statistical models with human–computer interaction, studying how large-scale human participation can improve computational pipelines, particularly those used for microbial phylogeny reconstruction, and studying gut microbiome.

My flagship citizen science project, Borderlands Science, is a puzzle game embedded within Borderlands 3. It has collected over 200 million puzzle solutions from 5 million players and was published in Nature Biotechnology in 2025, demonstrating that mass-market games can meaningfully advance biological research.

I previously earned my BSc in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology.
